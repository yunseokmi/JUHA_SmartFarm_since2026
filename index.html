🌱 Juha's Smart Grow Project

From a simple Arduino sensor to understanding how plants grow.

01 — Why I Started

My first Arduino project was a simple device that measured temperature and humidity.

At first, I was excited just to see the sensor work. But then I started wondering what I could actually do with the information.

Can a plant tell us when it actually needs water?

That question became the starting point of this project. I want to learn how sensors, data, and real plant observations can help me make better decisions.

03 — Questions I Want to Explore

  • When does a plant really need water?
  • How reliable is a soil moisture sensor?
  • Does sensor position affect the reading?
  • How does light affect plant growth?
  • Can technology make plant care more consistent?

04 — Connection to Eco Grow Club

I started Eco Grow Club at school to explore plants through growing, observation, sustainable design, journaling, and botanical art.

My Smart Grow Project connects technology with the same question I explore through the club:

How can we understand plants better and care for them more thoughtfully?

05 — Engineering Notebook
