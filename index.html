01. Origin / Why I Started

Before diving into plant automation, I spent approximately one year studying VEX robotics coding, followed by another year exploring Arduino hardware and microcontroller basics.

Starting with a simple temperature and humidity sensor project, I began asking a deeper question beyond just making the sensor work:

“Can a plant tell us when it actually needs water?”

This curiosity bridged my coding background with biological observation, turning a simple school assignment into a long-term engineering inquiry.

03. System Overview

Sensors → Arduino → Decision Logic → Pump → Plant → Data Record

The system operates in a closed feedback loop: environmental data is measured by sensors, processed by the Arduino microcontroller against defined thresholds, and triggers watering actions while logging performance data.

04. Build Log

Oct 2026 • Version v0.2

BUILD LOG 001: Testing soil moisture readings

Goal: Calibrate dry vs. wet thresholds in standard potting soil.

What I tried: Tested capacitive soil moisture sensors in dry soil and immediately after watering.

What I learned: Raw sensor values fluctuate depending on compaction and temperature. Calibration curves are necessary.

06. Experiments

Experiment ID: EXP-01

Research Question: Does sensor position relative to the plant stem change the soil moisture reading?

Result: Readings varied by up to 12% depending on proximity to the root ball vs. container edge.
